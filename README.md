A one-page broadsheet that sums up the world's news of **Wednesday 18 November 2026**:
the lead story is Iran widening its Gulf attacks to Kuwait and Bahrain, followed by
the Kerch bridge road span collapsing, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meeting in
Shenzhen ahead of Thursday's first-ever trilateral with Trump, and the Quad leaders
meeting in Manila. Ten more stories fill the page — Alaska's Senate race, Israel's
new speaker, Gaza's displaced families facing winter, Ebola in DR Congo, the US
losing measles-free status, Nvidia's record quarter, Russia's overnight drone
barrage on Ukraine, and Cambridge Dictionary's Word of the Year, "Human".

Every headline was checked against at least two independent news sources
(AP, Reuters, BBC, CNN, AFP) on 19 November 2026; claims that could not be
confirmed are attributed, not stated as fact.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is rendered by `build_poster.py` with Pillow from the
story list in that file — all text is drawn programmatically, so nothing is
garbled. `main.py` copies it byte-for-byte to the output directory.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `out/entry.png`, identical on every run.
